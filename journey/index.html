My Journey

From boards
to brands

From chip-level laptop repair to technology, business and digital marketing.

About the name: people call me Aju, and I’m from Kerala.

The path so far

  1. 2014 · Plus Two in CommerceI finished my Plus Two in Commerce.
  2. 2015–2017 · Starting out in chip-level repairI started working in chip-level repair. For about two years I worked in Kerala, Maharashtra and Salem in Tamil Nadu, and learned how to find and fix faults on the board.
  3. 2017–2025 · Working in QatarI worked at a computer shop in Qatar for about eight years. My main work there was laptop motherboard chip-level repair, MacBook repair, BIOS and IC programming, data recovery, and desktop and laptop hardware troubleshooting.
  4. 2025 · Studying businessI joined the BBA course at IGNOU. I’m in my second semester now.
  5. January 2026 · Learning digital marketingI like writing and making content, so I started learning digital marketing from YouTube and online courses. So far I’ve been learning SEO and content writing, AI website development, script writing, keyword research and social media marketing. This website is my first project.
  6. October 2026 · Moving to the UAEI moved to the UAE and started a business with a partner. We do laptop and computer repair, PlayStation repair, laptop spare parts, motherboard components, wholesale laptop accessories and used laptop trading.
