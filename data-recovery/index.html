Get your files back

When a laptop dies, most people worry more about their photos and documents than the laptop. The good news is that the files are often still there.

Because I repair boards at chip level, I can also reach data that other shops can’t, for example when the laptop won’t turn on, or when a MacBook SSD is fixed to the board.

What I recover data from

Laptop hard drives and SSDs

Drives that are not detected, very slow, or clicking.

Laptops that won’t turn on

If the board is the problem, I repair it first or take the data from the drive directly.

MacBook SSDs

USB drives and memory cards

Flash drives and cards that won’t open or aren’t recognised.

Deleted files

Formatted drives

Questions about data recovery

Can you recover data from a laptop that won’t turn on?

Often, yes. If the drive is fine, I take the data straight from it. If the fault is on the board, I may need to repair the board first to reach the data.

Can you recover data from a MacBook SSD?

In many cases, yes. Many MacBook SSDs are fixed to the logic board, so the board usually has to work before the data can be read. I check this first.

Do you recover data from USB drives and memory cards?

Yes. I check USB flash drives and memory cards that won’t open or aren’t detected.

Is recovery always possible?

No. Some drives are too badly damaged. I’ll tell you honestly after the free check, before you pay anything.

How much does data recovery cost?

The check is free. After I see the fault, I give you the price before I start.

Will my files stay private?

I only recover the files you ask for, and I give them back only to you.
