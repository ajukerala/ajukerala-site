About me

I started out as a chip-level computer technician. When a laptop comes in dead, I find the faulty part on the motherboard. You can’t guess your way through that. You test, rule things out, and keep going until you find the real cause.

Now I’m moving into digital marketing, and I use the same approach. When a website isn’t getting visitors, I look for the reason: slow pages, content that doesn’t match what people search for, or a structure Google can’t read well.

You can read the full story on my journey page. I’m new to this field, and I won’t pretend otherwise. What I can offer is a solid understanding of how websites and technology work, a habit of learning fast, and careful attention to detail. I use AI tools to speed up some tasks, but I check and edit everything myself.
