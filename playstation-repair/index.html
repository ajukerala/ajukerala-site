Console repair at board level

Game consoles have boards just like laptops: power circuits, HDMI chips and controllers. When one of these fails, many shops replace the whole board. I use the same chip-level skills I use on laptops to find the faulty part and fix it.

Before you buy a new console, let me check it. The check is free.

Console problems I fix

Not turning on

Press the power button and nothing, or it beeps and shuts off. Often a power fault or a short on the board.

No picture on TV

Console is on but the TV says no signal. Usually the HDMI port or the HDMI chip.

Overheating and loud fan

Gets hot and switches off, or the fan is very loud. Cleaning, new thermal paste and a fan check.

Disc and drive problems

Won’t read or eject discs. I check the drive and the parts around it.

Liquid damage

Something spilled on it? Switch it off and bring it quickly, before corrosion spreads.

Charging and port problems

Switch not charging or a controller port not working. I check the port and the charging circuit.

Questions about console repair

Which consoles do you repair?

PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox and Nintendo Switch.

My PS5 has no picture on the TV. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. No signal is often a damaged HDMI port or the HDMI chip on the board. I check both and replace what’s faulty.

My console turns on and then shuts off. What’s wrong?

It can be overheating, a power fault or a short on the board. I find the cause with a free check.

Do you clean consoles and change thermal paste?

Yes. A full clean and new thermal paste can stop overheating and make a loud fan quieter.

How much does console repair cost?

The check is free. I find the fault, then give you the price before I start any work.

Yes. Dubai and Sharjah. You can bring it, I can pick it up, or you can send it by courier.
