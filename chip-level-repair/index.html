MacBook and advanced chip-level repair

Most of my general motherboard work is on my laptop motherboard repair page. This page is for the jobs that need more specialist work: MacBook logic boards, BIOS and IC programming, and batteries.

I spent about eight years doing this kind of work at a computer shop in Qatar, including MacBook repair and BIOS programming. A lot of MacBooks that shops call “dead” only have one failed part.

What I repair

Specialist work, done at component level.

MacBook logic board repair

No power, no charging, liquid damage and shorts on MacBook boards. I find the failed part and replace it.

MacBook not charging

Often a charging chip or a fault in the USB-C circuit, not the charger. I test the circuit before replacing anything.

BIOS programming

Laptop stuck on the logo, black screen after an update, or boot loop. I reprogram the BIOS chip with a clean copy.

IC programming

Other chips on the board that need programming after a repair or replacement.

Laptop battery replacement

Fast drain or sudden shutdowns. I check the board first, so you only replace the battery when it’s really the cause.

Liquid damage

Questions about MacBook and chip-level repair

Do you repair MacBook logic boards?

Yes. I repair MacBook logic boards at chip level: power faults, charging faults, liquid damage and shorts. I find the failed part and replace it, instead of replacing the whole board.

What is BIOS and IC programming?

Some laptops stop booting because the BIOS chip’s software is damaged. I read the chip, write a clean copy back with a programmer, and the laptop can start again. I also program other ICs when needed.

Do you replace laptop batteries?

Yes, in Dubai and Sharjah. I check the charging circuit on the board first, so the battery is replaced only when it’s really the problem.

Do you work in Sharjah?

Yes. I help customers in Sharjah and Dubai. You can bring the laptop, I can pick it up, or you can send it by courier.

How much does it cost?

The check is free. I find the fault, then give you the price before I start any work.
