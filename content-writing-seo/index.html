Questions about SEO and content writing

What does SEO mean for a small business?

SEO helps your website show up on Google when people search for what you sell. For a small business, that means more calls and messages without paying for every click.

How long does SEO take to work?

Usually a few months. A new page can start showing within weeks, but ranking well takes time and steady work. I won’t promise fast results that I can’t control.

Can you write content for my website?

Yes. I write service pages, about pages, blog posts and product text in simple English, planned around what your customers search for.

Do you write social media posts too?

Yes. I plan posts, write captions and design simple graphics in Canva.

Which languages do you write in?

I write content in English. I can also talk with you in Malayalam, Hindi and basic Arabic.

How much do you charge?

The first consultation is free. After we talk, I give you a price based on the work.
